# AccordOS — Hackathon Pitch

## One line

AccordOS lets two companies delegate commercial bargaining to separate agents while deterministic policy rails and dual human approval keep authority under control.

## Problem

Routine renewals and supplier terms consume days of email while the actual bargaining space is often a small structured set of numbers and clauses. Generic agents are unsafe here because prompts cannot guarantee spending authority or confidentiality.

## Product

Each company privately sets floors, ceilings, priorities, and non-negotiable terms. Its agent sees only those limits plus the shared transcript. Every proposed offer is rejected or authorized by deterministic code. If one proposal satisfies both private profiles, people on both sides review and approve it.

## Web3 fit

After dual approval, AccordOS anchors a hash of the final agreement record to Solana devnet. Terms remain private; the timestamped proof is independently verifiable and tamper-evident.

## Demo script

1. Open the negotiation room and inspect Northstar’s private authority.
2. Start agents and watch three validated rounds.
3. Review the converged structured terms.
4. Approve once for each organization.
5. Connect Phantom or Solflare, sign the prepared devnet memo transaction, and watch AccordOS verify the confirmed hash before opening Solana Explorer.

## Business model

Free pilots establish trust. Team plans charge per active negotiation capacity. Enterprise plans add SSO, audit export, retention controls, KMS integration, and dedicated security review.

## Honest moat

The moat is not a chat interface. It is the authority model: structured templates, deterministic validation, tenant-confidential agent context, auditable negotiation state, and proof of an approved result.
